## Design Guidelines

### Color Scheme
- **Primary:** Professional blue (#1E3A8A) — Trust, stability
- **Accent:** Green (#10B981) — Success, growth, ROI
- **Alert:** Red (#EF4444) — Problems, urgency
- **Neutral:** Gray (#6B7280) — Supporting text

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, 32-44pt, Sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica)
- **Body:** Regular, 18-24pt, Sans-serif
- **Numbers:** Bold, larger size for emphasis
- **Consistent:** Use same font family throughout

### Visual Style
- **Clean & Modern:** Minimal design, plenty of white space
- **Data-Driven:** Charts, graphs, numbers prominently displayed
- **Professional:** High-quality graphics, consistent branding
- **Engaging:** Icons, colors, visual hierarchy

### Slide Transitions
- **Simple:** Fade or slide (not distracting)
- **Consistent:** Same transition throughout
- **Smooth:** Professional, not flashy
