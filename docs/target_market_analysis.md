# Target Market Analysis — Sell-Through Co-Pilot

## Executive Summary

Sell-Through Co-Pilot addresses a critical pain point for retailers and e-commerce businesses: **coordinating inventory, pricing, and promotions to maximize revenue while minimizing stockouts and overstock**. Our target buyers span multiple retail segments, from small online shops to large omnichannel retailers.

---

## Primary Target Segments

### 1. **E-Commerce & Online Retailers** ⭐ (Highest Priority)

**Why They Need It:**
- High promotional activity (flash sales, seasonal campaigns)
- Fast inventory turnover
- Multiple sales channels (website, marketplaces, social commerce)
- Ad spend directly impacts demand (PPC, social ads)
- Stockouts = lost revenue (no physical store backup)

**Examples:**
- **D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) brands**: Warby Parker, Glossier, Allbirds
- **Online marketplaces**: Sellers on Amazon, eBay, Etsy
- **Fashion e-commerce**: ASOS, Zara Online, Boohoo
- **Electronics e-commerce**: Newegg sellers, specialized tech retailers
- **Beauty & cosmetics**: Sephora online, Ulta online, indie beauty brands

**Pain Points We Solve:**
- Running out of stock during flash sales (wasted ad spend)
- Over-ordering before promotions (excess inventory, markdowns)
- Not coordinating ad spend with inventory availability
- Manual planning across channels

**Value Proposition:**
- "Never run out during your biggest sales"
- "Optimize ad spend based on inventory availability"
- "Reduce markdowns by 20-30%"

---

### 2. **Omnichannel Retailers** ⭐ (High Priority)

**Why They Need It:**
- Complex inventory across stores + online
- Promotions affect both channels differently
- Need to balance stock between locations
- Ad campaigns drive online traffic, but inventory may be in stores

**Examples:**
- **Fashion retailers**: H&M, Zara, Uniqlo, Gap
- **Electronics**: Best Buy, Target (electronics dept)
- **Home goods**: IKEA, Home Depot, Lowe's
- **Department stores**: Macy's, Nordstrom, Kohl's
- **Specialty retailers**: Sephora, Ulta, Lululemon

**Pain Points We Solve:**
- Online promotions when inventory is in stores (or vice versa)
- Store-level stockouts during regional campaigns
- Inefficient allocation between channels
- Ad spend driving traffic to unavailable products

**Value Proposition:**
- "Coordinate promotions across online + stores"
- "Allocate inventory based on ad-driven demand"
- "Reduce cross-channel stockouts by 40%"

---

### 3. **Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) / CPG** ⭐

**Why They Need It:**
- High volume, low margin
- Frequent promotions (weekly sales, BOGO)
- Seasonal spikes (holidays, back-to-school)
- Multiple SKUs (hundreds to thousands)
- Supplier lead times matter

**Examples:**
- **Grocery retailers**: Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, regional chains
- **Drugstores**: CVS, Walgreens, Rite Aid
- **Convenience stores**: 7-Eleven, Circle K
- **Warehouse clubs**: Costco, Sam's Club
- **CPG brands**: Selling through retailers (Nestlé, P&G, Unilever)

**Pain Points We Solve:**
- Weekly promotions causing stockouts
- Over-ordering seasonal items (leftover inventory)
- Not coordinating supplier orders with promo calendar
- Wasted ad spend on out-of-stock items

**Value Proposition:**
- "Optimize weekly promotions with inventory"
- "Reduce food waste / expired inventory"
- "Coordinate supplier orders with promo calendar"

## Target Buyer Personas

### Persona 1: "The E-Commerce Manager"
- **Role**: E-commerce Operations Manager, Inventory Manager
- **Company Size**: 50-500 employees, $10M-$100M revenue
- **Pain Points**: 
  - Running flash sales without enough inventory
  - Wasting ad spend on out-of-stock items
  - Manual coordination between inventory, pricing, and marketing
- **Decision Criteria**: ROI, ease of integration, time savings
- **Budget**: $5K-$50K/year

## Market Size Estimates

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- **Global retail market**: ~$30 trillion
- **E-commerce**: ~$6 trillion (20% of retail)
- **Addressable segment**: Retailers with $10M+ revenue, multi-SKU, promotional activity
- **Estimated TAM**: $500B-$1T in retail sales

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- **Retailers with**: 
  - 50+ SKUs
  - Regular promotions (monthly or more)
  - Ad spend > $10K/month
  - Inventory turnover > 4x/year
- **Estimated SAM**: $50B-$100B in retail sales

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- **Year 1-3 target**: 100-1,000 customers
- **Average deal size**: $25K-$250K/year
- **Estimated SOM**: $2.5M-$250M ARR
